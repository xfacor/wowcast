Advertiser terms

Last updated: November 15, 2026 · Versión en español

These terms apply to every campaign, sponsorship or ad purchased on wowcast (the "campaign"). By paying for a campaign, the advertiser accepts them.

1. What is purchased

  • Each plan or extra includes what is described under Plans and prices at the time of purchase, for the paid period (week or month).
  • Shared spaces (for example, the Featured plan header) rotate among several brands. Exclusive spaces are sold to a single brand per period and are assigned in order of payment.
  • wowcast does not guarantee a specific number of impressions, plays or sales, unless a visibility guarantee is agreed in writing (see section 7).
  • Country targeting: the advertiser can choose in which countries the campaign is shown (or not shown). It is included in the Showcase and Exclusive plans and is an extra in the Featured plan. It is approximate: it uses the country of each TV's internet connection, so someone using a VPN or traveling may see campaigns from another country. No viewer data is stored to do this. Exclusive spaces (such as the app-opening ad) are exclusive per country.

2. Price and payment

  • Prices are in US dollars (USD) and exclude taxes; applicable taxes will be added to the invoice where required.
  • Payment methods: PayPal (including cards) or bank transfer. Transfers in Dominican pesos are converted at the exchange rate of the payment date.
  • Campaigns are paid in advance. The campaign goes live once the payment is confirmed and the material is approved.
  • PayPal or bank fees charged to the advertiser are the advertiser's responsibility.
  • Launch offers (founding sponsor, "pay 3, get 4") cannot be combined and may end at any time without affecting campaigns already purchased.

3. Material provided by the advertiser

  • Link to the video or stream (.m3u8, .mp4, YouTube or another compatible format), a 16:9 background image (ideally 1920×1080), a title, a short description and the link the QR code should open (website, WhatsApp, store…).
  • Material must be sent at least 2 business days before the start date. If it arrives late, the campaign starts once approved, without extending the end date, unless agreed otherwise.
  • If the advertiser prefers, wowcast can design the artwork or edit the video as an extra.
  • Links must keep working during the campaign. If they stop working because of the advertiser, the lost time is not compensated.

4. Allowed content

  • The advertiser states that it owns the content or has the rights to broadcast it, and is responsible for any third-party claims.
  • Not accepted: adult content, betting or gambling, weapons, drugs or illegal products, misleading financial services, hate speech, misinformation, brand impersonation, or third-party content (movies, matches, channels) without permission.
  • wowcast reviews all material before it goes live and may request changes or reject a campaign. If it is rejected before it starts, 100% of the payment is refunded.
  • wowcast may pause a live campaign if it receives a well-founded complaint or finds that it breaches these terms.

5. Measurement and reports

  • Totals are reported by day and by country: impressions (times shown), plays, views over 30 seconds and QR code scans. The advertiser can check them at any time on its private results page.
  • Measurement is anonymous: wowcast does not collect or share viewers' personal data, IP addresses or identifiers. See the privacy policy.
  • Figures are indicative: some TVs may have statistics turned off or not connect every day, so the real reach may be higher than reported.
  • Reports are sent at the frequency of the purchased plan and when the campaign ends.

6. Changes, cancellations and refunds

  • During the campaign the material can be changed up to 2 times at no cost (sent 1 business day in advance).
  • Once the campaign has started there are no refunds for cancellation by the advertiser.
  • If the campaign is not shown because of a wowcast failure for more than 24 hours in a row, the lost days are compensated by extending the campaign or, if the advertiser prefers, with a proportional refund.
  • Cancellations before the start date are refunded minus payment fees already charged and, if done, the cost of design or editing.

7. Visibility guarantee

When a minimum number of impressions for the period is agreed in writing and the campaign does not reach it for reasons not attributable to the advertiser, wowcast will extend the campaign at no cost until it reaches that minimum or for the same length of time purchased, whichever comes first. It does not entitle the advertiser to a refund. If the campaign is limited to certain countries, the minimum is agreed taking into account the audience in those countries.

8. Liability

  • wowcast is a video player; it does not guarantee that every TV shows the campaign at all times (internet outages, TVs turned off, old app versions, etc.).
  • In any case, wowcast's liability is limited to the amount paid for the affected campaign.

9. Contact

To book a campaign, send material or ask anything: contacto@wowcast.app.
