How it works

  1. Send us your request with your brand, the plan you're interested in, the dates and what you want to show.
  2. We confirm availability and email you a payment link (PayPal or card).
  3. After paying, you send us the video or stream link, a 16:9 image (1920×1080) and the QR link. We review it and launch it on the agreed dates; TVs get it within hours, with no app update.
  4. You follow the results whenever you want on your private results page.

Privacy-friendly measurement

What we countDaily totals: how many times your ad was shown, how many times it was played, how many plays lasted over 30 seconds and how many QR scans it got.
CountryOnly the country (for example, US or DO): the one set on the TV, and for scans the one reported by the network. Never the city or exact location.
What we don't keepNo IP addresses, no device identifiers, no history of what each person watches. Data is stored already summed.

Allowed content

We accept brands, shops, events, media and creators with their own content or the rights to broadcast it. We don't accept adult content, gambling, illegal products, scams or third-party content without permission. We review every campaign and may reject any of them.
